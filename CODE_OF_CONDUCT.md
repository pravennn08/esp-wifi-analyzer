# Code of Conduct

ESP32 Wi-Fi Analyzer welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Everyone participating in this project should be able to ask
questions, share evidence, and contribute without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants respectfully, including people learning Rust,
  embedded development, or wireless diagnostics.
- Discuss the code, design, measurements, and reproducible behavior. Explain
  disagreements with evidence and constructive suggestions.
- Give actionable review feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect other people's time. Keep issues and reviews focused, and avoid
  pressuring volunteers for immediate responses.
- Respect privacy. Redact passwords, private network names, BSSIDs, device MAC
  addresses, and personal information when sharing diagnostic logs.
- Describe testing honestly. Distinguish simulations, host tests, local builds,
  CI results, and behavior observed on physical hardware.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, sexual harassment, or unwanted
  sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission, or encouraging
  others to target a person or their network.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, spamming project spaces, or continuing
  unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Using project discussions or contributions to organize harassment, expose
  private captured data, or coordinate disruption of other people's systems.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern or helping resolve one.

Technical disagreement, reporting a defect, and declining a contribution are
normal parts of development when expressed respectfully.

## Scope

This code applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
official project spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents
the project in another community. It does not grant maintainers authority over
unrelated private disagreements.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/pravennn08) using a private
contact method listed on their profile, if one is available. This project has
not designated a separate conduct-report email address.

If no private contact method is available, open an issue titled
`Request for confidential conduct contact`. Include only a request for a private
way to communicate; do not post incident details, names, evidence, or private
logs in the public issue. The maintainer can then arrange an appropriate channel.

When a private channel is established, provide the relevant links, a description
of the behavior, and any immediate concerns. Share only information necessary
to assess the incident. If the concern involves a maintainer, ask an uninvolved
maintainer to review it where one is available. GitHub's own reporting tools
remain available for behavior on its platform.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers should assess reports fairly, limit disclosure to information needed
to investigate or act, and avoid exposing a reporter's identity unnecessarily.
They should step back from decisions where a personal conflict prevents fair
review. The project cannot promise a particular response time or an independent
reviewer when no uninvolved maintainer is available.

Depending on severity, impact, and repeated behavior, responses may include:

1. A request to stop or correct the behavior.
2. A warning and a clear explanation of the expected conduct.
3. Editing or removing harmful content, or closing a disruptive discussion.
4. Temporary restrictions on participation.
5. Removal or a permanent ban for serious or repeated misconduct.

Serious incidents may warrant restrictions without earlier warnings. Maintainers
should explain decisions to affected participants when practical while protecting
private information. A participant may request reconsideration through the
arranged contact channel and provide relevant additional context.

Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards as other participants.
